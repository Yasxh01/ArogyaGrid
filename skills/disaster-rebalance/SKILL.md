# Skill: Disaster Resource Rebalancing & Logistics Optimization

## Overview
Standard operating procedure for identifying donor Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and generating cross-district transfer recommendations during localized health crises.

## Rules & Constraints
1. **Donor Protection**: A donor PHC must maintain at least 14 days of its own projected supply after transfer.
2. **Radius Constraint**: Prioritize donor PHCs within 45 km radius before searching adjacent districts.
3. **Escrow & Chain of Custody**: Generate a secure cryptographic transfer token verified upon physical receipt.
